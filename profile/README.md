# 🧙‍♂️🌍 Wogwon Society
**Not-for-Profit Media & Culture Collective**

Welcome to **Wogwon Society**! We're a community of creators, developers, and designers passionate about building tools, content, and resources that make multimedia accessible to everyone – both online and offline.

## 💡 What We Do
- **Create** innovative tools and resources
- **Upgrade** systems for accessibility and inclusion
- **Release** multimedia content that empowers communities

Our goal is simple: **A better world for all, without discrimination.**

## 🎉 Join the Movement
Founded by [Islati](https://github.com/Islati) in 2024, Wogwon Society welcomes all creators who believe in the power of **community, creativity, and compassion**.
